🔍 How Tamper Detection Works with audit_live.log:

1. Automatic Anchoring: Whenever a log line is added to audit_live.log, the background worker automatically hashes it with SHA-256 and calls appendEntryHash() on the smart contract.

2. File Editing Detection: Open audit_live.log on your computer in VS Code or Notepad. Change any word or number (e.g. from $5000.00 to $9999.00) and save (Ctrl+S).

3. Instant Hash Mismatch: Within 1.5 seconds, the dashboard reads the modified line, calculates its new hash, and compares it against the Ethereum block. Since the hashes do not match, it immediately flags 🚨 TAMPER DETECTED in red!
